About

Browser games are
the real craft

I'm a front-end developer who has spent 5+ years building HTML5 games — real ones, where every frame counts. At Evolution, I shipped production slot titles in an iGaming environment: 60fps under budget constraints, real-money pressure, zero tolerance for bugs.

That rigor means I understand custom rendering pipelines, object pooling, texture atlases, and what it takes to squeeze performance out of a browser. I now want to bring that craft to games people play for fun — casual titles, playable ads, and indie/mobile projects.

I also collaborate with my wife — a 2D artist — on game jam projects and co-run a small studio side venture. I'm expanding into Unity / C# and excited to work with studios who care as much about polish as I do.
